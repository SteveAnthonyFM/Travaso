Travaso for Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 26 or later.

**What's new in 1.0.1:** a safety fix so converting `Song.MP3` to MP3 can never replace the original (the
name-conflict question now defaults to Keep Both), stricter handling of damaged or unusual files, tidy-up of
temporary files when you quit mid-batch, and a more locked-down release process. Full list in the changelog.

This build is not notarized yet, so macOS will warn you the first time: open the DMG, drag
Travaso to Applications, try to open it, then go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click
Open Anyway next to the Travaso message.
